# How To Grade & Clean Your Sports Cards Before Submitting

## RULES TAUGHT
- Whitening along an edge is edge condition/wear. [02:36-03:07]
- A white rounded corner is corner wear. [02:36-03:07]
- Off-centering is not a deal-breaker on a card with no borders. [05:11-05:41]
- A gem-mint reference card shows sharp edges and corners with no whitening anywhere. [06:46-07:16]
- The instructor deducts half a point per defect category to reach a grade. [02:36-03:07]
