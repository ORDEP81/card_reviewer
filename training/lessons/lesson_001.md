# How to Find Clean Raw Cards to Grade on eBay

## RULES TAUGHT

- Corner touches — rounded, raised, or touched — hold a card back from PSA 10. [05:11-05:22]
- Edges should run straight and clean all the way around; chipped or nicked edges are a defect. [05:22-05:36]
- Surface is the hardest attribute to judge, and surface flaws frequently cannot be
  seen in listing photographs at all. [05:36-05:56]
- A dimple looks different in a scan than in person; scan appearance must be learned
  as its own skill. [05:36-05:56]
- PSA's stated front-centering standard is 55/45 or better. [04:16-04:28]
