# MLP Evaluating Condition SOP - Corners

## RULES TAUGHT
- Corners are the most common defect, because cards move unprotected in packs and are thin. [00:00-00:40]
- The three corner defects, in descending order of frequency: whitening/chipping, rounding, bending. [00:45-00:55]
- A coloured corner shows chipping more readily than a white one, because a white corner cannot whiten. [01:00-01:20]
- Corners must be sharp right angles; rounding is grade-limiting. [01:30-02:00]
- Thick cards damage more easily at the corners. [02:12-02:19]

## VISUAL EXAMPLES
The instructor shows a whitened corner, a rounded corner against a sharp reference corner, and a
slightly lifted corner. Frames not individually sampled for this lesson; the ordering claim and
the coloured-corner mechanism rest on narration.
