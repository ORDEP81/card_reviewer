# MLP Evaluating Condition SOP - Edges

## RULES TAUGHT
- Edge defects are the least common of the four condition categories. [00:05-00:15]
- Whitening is essentially the only edge defect to look for; edges do not round or bend. [00:35-00:55]
- Coloured edges reveal whitening; white edges cannot. [00:20-00:35]
- Thicker cards damage at the edges more easily. [00:55-01:10]
