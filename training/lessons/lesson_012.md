# PSA published centering standard for a PSA 10

## RULES TAUGHT
PSA's documented requirement for a Gem Mint 10 is 55/45 or better centering on the
front and 75/25 or better on the back.

## PROVENANCE
Stated by the project owner (2026-08-29) as PSA's own documentation. Recorded as a
reference-mode source rather than a video citation: no training video in this corpus
states the 75/25 back figure. One video does state the 55/45 front figure
(lesson_001, yt_s46CrXjVwCc 04:16-04:28), and that instructor is now confirmed correct.

## WHY THIS LESSON EXISTS
It corrects a promotion error. During the review of the first 32 candidates:

- CENTERING_PSA_TOLERANCE_001, which asserted the 55/45 front standard, was REJECTED
  as unverified. It was correct.
- CENTERING_60_40_FLOOR_001, which asserts 60/40 is a workable floor for a 10, was
  ACCEPTED because two independent instructors said so. It contradicts the documented
  standard.

The failure mode is worth recording: agreement between two practitioners was treated as
corroboration, while the accurate claim appeared once, unsourced, and was discarded.
Independent sources repeating each other is not the same as verification.

## RESOLUTION
CENTERING_PSA10_STANDARD_001 is promoted as `objective` and supersedes
CENTERING_60_40_FLOOR_001. CENTERING_FRONT_BACK_TOLERANCE_001 (directionally correct
but imprecise) is superseded by the same rule, which carries the actual figure.

## POTENTIAL CONTRADICTIONS
CENTERING_PRODUCT_LENIENCY_001 remains active and states PSA extends centering leeway on
products known to be cut poorly. That is a claim about observed grading practice, not
about the documented standard, so the two coexist. If practice and standard are ever
found to conflict directly, the standard governs.
