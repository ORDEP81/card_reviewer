# Why You NEED These Tools When Grading Cards

## RULES TAUGHT
- 60/40 front centering is the bare minimum, and at that ratio everything else must be perfect. [00:26-00:52]
- A single faint print line on the FRONT is a personal disqualification. [01:45-02:11]
- Print lines on the BACK have still received 10s. [01:45-02:11]
- Inspection checklist: centering, corners, edges, surface, then repeat on the back. [02:59-03:20]
- A flaw visible to the naked eye means an automatic 9 or worse. [02:59-03:20]
