# MLP Evaluating Condition SOP - Surface

## RULES TAUGHT
- Surface is the hardest subgrade to judge and is not easy to see in photographs. [00:05-00:20]
- Surface is partly cleanable; light scratches may come off before submission. [00:20-00:35]
- Shinier and foil surfaces scratch more easily and are harder to grade. [00:35-01:00]
- The three surface defects are factory/print lines, dimples, and scratches. [01:00-01:50]
- A print line running across the card will be penalised every time. [01:10-01:25]

## VISUAL EXAMPLES
A Luka Doncic card with a factory line running horizontally between mouth and nose; a dimple; a
scratch. Frames not individually sampled.

## INSTRUCTOR OPINIONS
The instructor mentions a script for questioning sellers about condition. That is a sourcing
tactic, not condition knowledge, and is not recorded - though it corroborates the existing
image-limitations rules about photographs being insufficient.

## POTENTIAL CONTRADICTIONS

outcome rather than a personal policy. Both may be promoted; the pair is complementary.
